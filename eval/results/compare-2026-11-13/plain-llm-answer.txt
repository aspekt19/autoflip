### Conditions before buying
1. **Clean title and no reported accidents:** Review Carfax **and** AutoCheck if available. Neither guarantees an accident-free vehicle; have an independent shop inspect for collision repairs, structural damage and hail damage. Reject salvage, rebuilt or flood history.
2. **Subaru-specialist inspection:** Have them check the CVT, leaks, wheel bearings, suspension, brakes and four matching tires with closely matched tread depths.
3. **Thermo control valve:** This is a known issue on these Foresters. Ask a Subaru dealer to check the VIN for repair history and applicable extended coverage.
4. **Safety equipment:** Verify EyeSight works, any windshield replacement was properly calibrated, and recalls are complete.
5. **No forced add-ons:** Decline mandatory protection packages, tracking devices and other extras that break the budget.
